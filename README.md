# CS-330-Computer-Graphics-and-Visualization
CS 330 Computer Graphics and Visualization portfolio project

## How do I approach designing software?

Working on my 3D scene helped me develop a better understanding of how important planning and organization are when designing software. I learned how to take a real-world object and break it down into simpler shapes that could be recreated in a 3D environment. For my project, I used basic OpenGL meshes and adjusted their scale, rotation, and position to recreate the layout of my selected reference image.

My design process involved building the scene in smaller sections instead of trying to complete everything at once. I added objects, tested them, and then made adjustments before moving forward. I also worked with textures, materials, lighting, and camera controls to improve the appearance and usability of the scene. In future projects, I can use this same approach by planning the major components first, breaking difficult problems into smaller pieces, and testing each part as I develop it.

## How do I approach developing programs?

One of the main development strategies I used during this project was iteration. Throughout the milestones, I continuously added to and improved my 3D scene. I first focused on creating and positioning the basic objects. Later, I added textures, camera controls, lighting, materials, and projection options.

Testing after each change was especially important because even small adjustments to coordinates, lighting values, transformations, or textures could have a large effect on the final scene. I also learned how useful reusable functions can be for keeping code organized. Functions for transformations, textures, materials, and lighting helped make the program easier to understand and modify.

My approach to coding evolved throughout the course. At the beginning, working with OpenGL and understanding transformations was challenging. As I continued through the milestones, I became more comfortable reading existing code, identifying where changes needed to be made, and troubleshooting problems when the scene did not look the way I expected.

## How can computer science help me in reaching my goals?

Computational graphics and visualization gave me new skills that I can continue using throughout my education in computer science. This course helped me strengthen my understanding of programming, problem-solving, debugging, coordinates, transformations, textures, lighting, materials, and working with an existing codebase.

Professionally, this experience can also help me as I work toward a career in software development. Even if I do not work specifically in computer graphics, the programming and troubleshooting skills I developed are transferable to many areas of software engineering. Completing this project also gives me another example of a larger programming project that I can include in my GitHub portfolio to demonstrate my ability to learn new technologies, improve code through iteration, and complete a working application.
